Automation used to be brittle. If an email subject line didn't match the rule exactly, the automation failed. AI has changed that. With LLMs, we can build "fuzzy" automation that understands intent.

## The New Stack: Zapier + OpenAI

Zapier now lets you use ChatGPT as a step in your workflows. This effectively gives you an AI agent that can read, think, and act.

### Use Case 1: The Smart Inbox Assistant

**Trigger:** New Email in Gmail.
**Action 1 (ChatGPT):** "Read this email. If it is a sales pitch, reply with 'Not interested'. If it is an invoice, extract the amount and due date. If it is urgent, summarize it."
**Action 2 (Slack):** Send the summary to me on Slack.
**Action 3 (Notion):** Add the invoice details to my database.

This workflow doesn't just move data; it *processes* it.

### Use Case 2: Social Media Repurposing

**Trigger:** New Blog Post (RSS).
**Action 1 (ChatGPT):** "Read this blog post and write a LinkedIn post, a Twitter thread, and an Instagram caption."
**Action 2 (Buffer):** Schedule these posts.

## Getting Started

You don't need to be a coder. Zapier's new "AI Copilot" lets you describe the automation you want in plain English, and it builds the workflow for you.

Automation is no longer about saving seconds; it's about freeing up your brain space.
