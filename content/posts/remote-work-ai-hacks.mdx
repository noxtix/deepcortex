Remote work offers freedom, but it also brings distractions. Fortunately, a new wave of AI tools is designed specifically to keep you focused and professional. Here are our top picks.

## 1. Silence the World with Krisp
Working from a cafe? **Krisp.ai** uses AI to cancel background noise in real-time. It doesn't just mute the noise from *your* mic; it also filters the incoming audio so you can hear your colleagues clearly.

## 2. Never Take Meeting Notes Again
Tools like **Otter.ai** and **Fireflies.ai** join your Zoom/Teams calls automatically. They record, transcribe, and most importantly, *summarize* the meeting. You get a list of action items delivered to your inbox minutes after the call ends.

## 3. Fix Your Eye Contact
**Captions.ai** (and now NVIDIA Broadcast) offers "Eye Contact" correction. It re-renders your eyes to look at the camera, even if you are reading a script on your screen. It’s subtle, but it builds trust.

## 4. Draft Emails in Seconds
Use **Superhuman** or **Spark**'s AI features. Jot down "Agree, but ask for Tuesday", and the AI expands it into a polite, full-paragraph email.

## 5. Focus Music
**Brain.fm** and **Endel** generate soundscapes that scientifically boost focus. Unlike a Spotify playlist, this music is designed to blend into the background and stimulate neural phase locking.

Remote work isn't about working longer; it's about working smarter. These tools give you your time back.
